Our story

Built in The Hague, sailed worldwide

Since 1975 Nacra has built catamarans for every kind of sailor, from relaxed cruising to Olympic racing. Our home is Scheveningen, The Hague, and our boats, sails and parts reach sailors everywhere through the webshop and our dealer network.

Waarom bij ons

  1. Since 1975

    Nearly five decades of catamaran building, from beach cats to racing machines.

  2. Olympic pedigree

    The Nacra 17 is the Olympic mixed multihull class, so you sail what the best sail.

  3. Hand-crafted sails

    Designed and made by Performance Sails, Nacra's own sail loft.

  4. Everything for your boat

    Parts, rigging, hardware, covers and more, available straight from the webshop.
